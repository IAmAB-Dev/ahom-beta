1. What Ahom is

Ahom is a desktop email client published by Plenith LLC. It connects to email accounts you already have (Gmail, Microsoft 365 / Outlook, iCloud, Fastmail, generic IMAP / SMTP, and similar providers) and renders your mail locally on your device.

Ahom is distributed as a code-signed binary. During the public beta it is offered for Windows, with macOS and Linux to follow. There is no Ahom server in the data path — every network call goes from your device directly to the mail provider you connected, or to an AI endpoint you chose. Plenith LLC does not operate a backend that proxies, stores, or relays your mail. How the app handles your information is described in the Privacy statement.

6. AI features

Ahom includes AI features (thread summary, draft suggestion, natural-language search, classification). By default these run against a language model on your own machine, and nothing leaves the device. If you deliberately point the app at a remote AI endpoint (your own API key with a third-party provider), payloads for that feature will be sent to that endpoint when you invoke it, and a per-feature badge surfaces that before any remote call.
