The whole point

There is no Ahom cloud.

Most mail apps quietly route your inbox through their servers. Ahom doesn't have any. Everything runs where you can see it — on the machine in front of you.

Encrypted on your disk

Messages, attachments, and the search index sit in one encrypted database (SQLCipher, AES-256). The key is sealed in your OS keychain — never on our side, because there is no our side.

AI that never leaves

Summaries, drafts, and natural-language search run against a language model on your own machine. Your email is never sent to a cloud AI service.

Nothing phones home

No telemetry, no analytics, no crash pings, no ad SDKs. The binary you install doesn't call us. There's nothing to opt out of, because it was never collecting.

A full inbox, done quietly

Everything you expect — and the parts that protect you.

Ahom is a complete mail client, not a privacy demo. It just happens to keep the surveillance out.

On-device AI

Summarize a long thread, ask a plain-English question about a message, or get a smart reply — all running on a local model on your machine. Point it at your own LM Studio or Ollama if you'd rather, or use the bundled engine. The default sends nothing anywhere.

  • Thread summaries
  • Ask a message
  • Smart replies
  • Natural-language search

Smart triage

Mail sorts itself into Priority, Personal, Newsletters, Watch and Quiet — by an on-device classifier that learns from your corrections, not from a server profiling you.

Security built in

Phishing detection, sender authentication with DMARC and verified-sender logos (BIMI), tracking-pixel blocking, remote-image gating, and PGP encrypt / decrypt when you and your correspondent both have keys.

  • DMARC + BIMI
  • Pixel blocking
  • Phishing checks
  • PGP

A calm reader

Formatted mail renders faithfully — no dark-on-dark surprises. Preview PDF, image and text attachments inline, and print or save any message as a typeset PDF.

  • Inline attachment preview
  • Print / Save-as-PDF
  • Snooze & send-later

Your accounts, your keys

Bring the mail you already have.

Connect several accounts at once, each colour-coded. Credentials are sealed in your operating system's keychain — never in a config file, never on a server.
